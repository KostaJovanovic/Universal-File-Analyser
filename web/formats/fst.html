What is a .FST file?

.FST is a file format that Analyser can open and analyse in your browser.

Did you know
  • Analyser handles .FST alongside related formats such as .MOD, .XM, .IT and more.
  • In Analyser's format library, .FST sits in the System & disk category.
  • Analyser opens .FST files right in your browser - they are parsed and rendered on your device, never uploaded to a server.
What Analyser shows you
Play and analyse tracker modules - MOD, XM, IT, S3M and around sixty relatives from the demoscene and the Amiga and DOS eras. A module is not recorded audio: it is a bank of instrument samples plus a pattern score saying which sample to trigger, on which channel, at which row, so playing one means running a tracker engine. Analyser renders the song on your device with libopenmpt - the engine behind OpenMPT - and then treats the result like any other audio, so you get the full Sound section: waveform, spectrogram, transport, loudness, key and BPM. Above it sits the module itself - the tracker that wrote it, the channel, pattern and order counts, the song message, and the sample and instrument name lists, which is traditionally where authors left their greetings. The rendered song can be saved as a WAV.
Open a .FST file
Drag a .FST file onto the Analyser home page (or tap to pick one). It opens entirely in your browser - nothing is uploaded, there is no account, and it works offline once installed.

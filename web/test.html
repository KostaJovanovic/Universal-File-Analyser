Cards & rows.

The analysis card is the workhorse: an .anr-card filled with table.anr-readout label/value rows. Cards are static, always-open sections by default; a card opted in with .anr-collapsible gets a JS-wired direct-child h3 that toggles it (with a +/- glyph) and is the pattern used for sections that render closed by default.

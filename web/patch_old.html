7.0

X-Ray

22 July 2026

  • Seventh milestone: Analyser sees straight through a file. A deep forensic pass reads the hidden internals of every photo, video and sound, and explains all of it in plain English.

Photos

  • NewSpot a doctored photo. A new Advanced section brings image forensics to any JPEG. Error-level analysis highlights areas saved at a different quality, and JPEG ghosts reveal regions compressed a second time.
  • NewFind data hidden inside an image. Bit-plane analysis inspects the parts of a picture the eye cannot see, estimates how likely it is that a message is concealed there, and lets you browse every bit plane.
  • NewRead a photo’s edit history and hidden identity. Analyser lays out the edit-history trail some files record, and gathers GPS location, camera serial and owner name into a plain privacy report.
  • NewAnalyse a RAW photo two ways. A camera RAW file holds both a ready-made preview and the untouched sensor data. A switch at the top of the results lets you analyse either one.
  • NewDetect codes, and pull every thumbnail. Barcodes and QR codes in a picture are now read automatically, and all the preview images buried inside a file can be extracted at once.

Video

  • NewSee exactly how a video file is built. A new Advanced card opens up an MP4 or MOV, showing the tree of its building blocks, every track it holds, and the signs that separate a camera original from a re-saved file.
  • NewRead a camera’s flight recorder. Action-camera and phone footage often carries a hidden telemetry track. Analyser decodes GoPro and CAMM data, drawing the GPS route on a local map and plotting the sensor traces.
  • NewFingerprint the encoder and check the stream. Analyser reads deep into the compressed video to recover its true profile, colour and range, and checks that against what the container claims. It also recovers the exact x264/x265 settings left in the stream.
  • NewA whole video at a glance. A content timeline squeezes the video into a single colour “barcode”, with a brightness curve and flags for black or frozen stretches. Click anywhere on it to jump the player there.
  • FasterPlay unsupported video sooner. A clip the browser cannot play is converted to a fast, lightweight copy by default. An Advanced panel sets resolution, frame rate and speed, including an extra-quick Turbo mode.
  • FixAnalysis always describes the original. That playable copy is used only for playback. Every result is read from the original file you dropped, never the converted stand-in.
  • FixMotion timelines play for any video. The synced motion and gyro timelines play even when the video uses a codec the browser cannot open, by following the same playable copy as the main player.

Sound

  • NewA broadcast-grade loudness meter. A full EBU R128 readout shows integrated, momentary and short-term loudness, the range between quiet and loud passages, and the real true peak that ordinary meters miss.
  • NewCatch a fake “lossless” file. Analyser inspects the frequency picture of a FLAC, WAV or ALAC and flags the ceiling left behind when it was really made from an MP3. It also reports the effective bit depth.
  • NewHear the key, and the hidden tones. The musical key is estimated alongside the tempo. Forensic listeners flag mains hum, scan for inaudible ultrasonic tones, and decode touch-tone phone digits.
  • NewSignal-health checks. New crest factor (how heavily a master is compressed) and DC offset (a capture-fault tell) readings join the file info.
  • NewRemove background noise from a recording. A denoise option sits beside the vocal tools on any audio file. An on-device AI model strips out hiss and shows the result as a Clean to Noise blend you can play, analyse or save.
  • NewFriendlier one-tap sound filters. The instant isolate presets are now Underwater, Radio and Hollow. The true AI vocal split sits under a clearly labelled AI separation button.
  • NewAll the deep readings in one Advanced section. The loudness meter and the spectral forensics gather into a single folded Advanced section, the way photos and videos already do. The everyday readings stay up top.
  • NewA video’s sound, analysed in full. Pulling the sound out of a video runs the same analysis as opening that sound on its own, rather than the shorter summary it used to show.
  • FixSwitching AI tool mid-prompt now works. Choosing a different model while a download prompt was open used to be ignored. It updates the prompt straight away now.

Clearer help

  • NewAnswers in plain English, everywhere. The hundreds of little [?] explanations across the site have been rewritten so anyone can follow them. Many readouts and headings that never had one now carry it.
  • NewTidier help that stays put. Tapping a [?] opens a small pop-up anchored to the button instead of pushing the page down, and clicking anywhere else closes it.
  • FixA clear answer for an empty file. Dropping a 0-byte file now explains plainly that there is nothing inside to open, instead of failing with a puzzling error.

Elsewhere

  • FixA clearer 3D mesh verdict. The Mesh integrity card now shows a plain Watertight or Faults found badge in its heading, with friendlier wording for what a “non-manifold” edge actually means.
